Credit Card Approval Prediction System

Predicting credit card approval is a vital risk-management task for financial institutions. This system employs an advanced machine learning classification pipeline trained on real-world banking datasets containing demographic, occupational, financial, and credit historical metrics of 36,000+ applicants.

Classification & Risk Strategy

To establish a robust decision-making matrix, the target variable (approved/rejected) was defined by evaluating each applicant's monthly payment history status code:

  • Approved Status (0): Month marked as closed, no loan, or 1-29 days past due.
  • Rejected Status (1): Month marked as 30+ days, 60+ days, 90+ days, or severe overdue / write-off.

Since class distribution is heavily imbalanced (only a small percentage of accounts exhibit overdue status), training utilized custom weight ratios (e.g. scale_pos_weight in XGBoost and class_weight='balanced' in Random Forest) to optimize recall on negative/high-risk profiles.
